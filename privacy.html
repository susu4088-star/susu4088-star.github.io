Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

What this app is

"okdoki" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Okdoki YouTube channel (@Okdoki11) to upload videos, set thumbnails and manage video details on that channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to read basic information about the owner's own channel (channel ID, title, video list) and to upload videos and thumbnails to it. It does not access any other user's data.

How data is stored

The OAuth token granted by the channel owner is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never shared or sold. No analytics, cookies or tracking are used on this website.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
